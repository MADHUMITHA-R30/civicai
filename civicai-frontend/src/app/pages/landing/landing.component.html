Why CivicAI?

🎙️

Voice-to-Text Reporting

Designed for low literacy citizens. Speak naturally in simple words; AI transcribes and extracts details automatically.

🔍

Semantic Duplicate Detection

AI identifies similar complaints nearby, links them into issue clusters CIV-2026-XXXX, preventing duplicate municipal workload.

⚡

Explainable Priority AI

Calculates severity score (0-100) based on location near school/hospital, hazard level, duration, and safety impact.

📸

Before/After Proof

Department officials must upload after-resolution photos before marking issues resolved. Interactive slider comparison.

🛡️

Citizen Verification Layer

Citizens verify if the problem was actually fixed. Disputed resolutions trigger auto-alerts back to officials.

📈

Recurring Hotspot Analytics

Identifies recurring problem areas over months and recommends permanent infrastructure intervention.
